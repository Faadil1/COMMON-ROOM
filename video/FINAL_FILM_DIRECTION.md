# COMMON ROOM — Day 17 Cinematic Film Direction

Branch: `film/day17-cinematic`

## Purpose

This film must **sell the product idea**, not document browser navigation.

The website is source material.
The film is a designed artifact.

Primary statement:

> You do not receive a card. You accumulate one.

Canonical motion law:

`MARK → LAYER → ACCUMULATE → APERTURE → REVEAL → BELONG`

## Format

- 16:9
- 1920×1080
- 30 fps
- 55–65 seconds
- Remotion as master timeline
- HTML/GSAP “HyperFrames-style” sequences for kinetic typography, masks, maps and transitions
- Real product UI used only as clean isolated proof
- No raw browser chrome
- No cursor-led walkthrough as the dominant grammar

## Signature visual device

### THE ACCESSION APERTURE

The aperture is the film's transition system.

It can:
- crop a room into view;
- cut through card layers;
- reveal hidden archive material;
- become the Member Lens;
- multiply into wall cards;
- resolve into streets in the Quarter.

The aperture must feel like one physical law crossing the whole film.

## Persistent motion device

### THE TRACE SPINE

A thin trace travels through the film.

It begins as:
- a margin line,
then becomes:
- registration mark,
- reference path,
- street line,
- card edge,
- civic map.

This creates visual causality instead of unrelated transitions.

Controlled 3D inspection.

### 00:31–00:39 — MEMBER LENS

The aperture expands to fill the frame.

### 00:56–01:02 — END

Return to ivory.

## Product proof policy

Real runtime must prove:
- Member Record
- ACCESSION STRATA
- card back / QR
- aging / renewal marks
- Member Lens
- Living Collection
- Quarter reveal

But the runtime should be isolated into:
- transparent or cropped product inserts;
- card renders;
- short deterministic clips;
- image sequences.

Never show a browser wandering through the entire product.

## Motion rules

1. Motion must have a job.
2. Aperture is the primary wipe/mask.
3. Trace spine is the primary continuity line.
4. Depth appears only when evidence becomes physical.
5. No generic SaaS easing.
6. No neon, glassmorphism or cinematic-tech blue.
7. No random zooms.
8. No decorative particles.
9. No fake 3D for sections that are conceptually flat.
10. Use silence/negative space between major reveals.

## Sound design

TTS is not enough.

Layers:
- quiet room tone / paper air
- registration click
- paper edge movement
- index-card tick
- stamp impact for RENEWED
- soft low-frequency body for strata separation
- subtle scanner/optic tone for Member Lens
- restrained collective swell for Living Collection
- final room-tone drop before the last line

Narration remains calm and documentary, not commercial-hype.
